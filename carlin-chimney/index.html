What an inspection looks at.

The National Fire Protection Agency recommends annual inspection and/or cleaning of all fireplaces, wood burning stoves and chimneys.

  1. Cap & damper topKeeps rain, animals and debris out of the flue.
  2. CrownCracks or deterioration here are a common leak source.
  3. Mortar jointsDeteriorating joints get tuck pointed.
  4. FlashingWhere the chimney meets the roof.
  5. Flue linerCracked clay or rusted metal lets heat and gases escape.
  6. Creosote & sootFlammable buildup inside the flue.
  7. FireboxRepaired and rebuilt when it fails.
  8. Oil & gas appliance fluesHeating system venting, not just fireplaces.

Inside your panel.

Every circuit in the house starts here. When we repair or replace a panel, these are the parts we're working on.

AService entrance
Where the utility feed comes in from the meter.
BMain breaker
Shuts off power to the whole house at once.
CBranch breakers
One per circuit: kitchen, bedrooms, the EV charger.
DNeutral bar
The return path for each circuit's white wire.
EGround bar
Bare copper grounds, tied to the grounding system.

Questions, answered.

Where do you work?

Ocean and Monmouth counties and the surrounding area for service work, from Toms River and Brick to Freehold, Red Bank and Long Branch. New construction projects anywhere in New Jersey.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Retail buildings, restaurants, offices and shopping centers, as well as homes.

Can you install an EV charger or a whole home generator?

Both. Each one is its own circuit, so we look at the panel first to make sure it has room.
